Nordic Sodium Cell Registry, 2025 edition

Compiled by the Nordic Battery Forum. The registry lists sodium-based cells whose developers submitted test data during 2024 and 2025. Figures are as reported by the developers and have not been independently verified unless an independent test site is named. Cycle life is given to 80 percent of initial capacity at a 1C rate unless stated otherwise.

Entry 1: SodaCore-X. Developer: Seaborg Energy. Chemistry: room-temperature sodium-sulfur. Electrolyte: NaFE-2, supplied by Ostrand Chemicals. Tested at: Oskarsund Battery Test Centre. Reported cycle life: 2,590 cycles. Cell-level energy density: 275 watt-hours per kilogram. Projected cost: 50 US dollars per kilowatt-hour.
